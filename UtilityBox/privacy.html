Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Applies to UtilityBox for macOS (bundle ID es.rmartinl1109.utilitybox)

In short: the developer collects no personal data. UtilityBox has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no accounts. Its tools run on your Mac, and the few that contact an online service only do so when you use them, as listed in section 3.

1. Data we collect

None. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app, we do not use third-party SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account.

2. Data processed on your Mac

  • Network and system tools (network scanner, ping, port checker, DNS lookup, system and battery monitors, disk analyzer): results are held in memory while the app is open and are not sent to us.
  • Files you choose (images, audio, video, QR codes, hashes, Base64): converted, compressed or analyzed locally. Files are only read from, and written to, locations you pick or the Downloads and Music folders. The disk analyzer remembers folders you authorize using macOS security-scoped bookmarks stored on your Mac.
  • Voice recorder: audio is recorded to files on your Mac after you grant microphone access, and is never uploaded.
  • Preferences: language, theme and favorites are stored locally in the app's preferences.
  • License state: the start of your free trial and the date of your purchase are stored in your Mac's Keychain so the trial cannot be reset by deleting preferences. This information never leaves your Mac.

These connections are started by you when you run the related tool. We do not receive any of them; they go directly to the services below, which have their own privacy policies.

4. Permissions the app may request

  • Local network: to discover and diagnose devices on your network.
  • Microphone: only in the voice recorder, and only after you start a recording.
  • Files and folders: only those you select in a file panel, plus the Downloads and Music folders for exported results.

The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox. You can revoke any permission at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

5. Purchases

The free trial and the one-time lifetime license are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your Apple ID, payment method or billing details.

6. Third-party tracking and advertising

UtilityBox contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking technologies, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you share crash or usage diagnostics with developers through macOS, Apple may provide us anonymized, aggregated reports.

7. Children

UtilityBox is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Data created by the app lives on your Mac and you can remove it at any time by deleting the app and its files. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Changes that affect the data the app handles will also be reflected in the app's privacy manifest and in its App Store privacy details.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email utilitybox.support@rmartinl1109.com or visit our support page.
