Contact & Support

Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Applies to UtilityBox for macOS (bundle ID es.rmartinl1109.utilitybox)

Need help, found a bug or have an idea for a new utility? Write to us — we usually reply within 24–48 hours.

Contact

Email: utilitybox.support@rmartinl1109.com

You can also open UtilityBox › Settings and use the built-in feedback options, which prepare a message with your app version and macOS version (no files or personal data are attached).

When you write to us, please include

  • Your Mac model and macOS version (UtilityBox requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later).
  • The UtilityBox version shown in Settings.
  • The tool you were using and the steps to reproduce the problem.
  • For permission issues, whether access was granted in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

All tools are available for 7 days. Afterwards you can unlock UtilityBox with a single one-time purchase; there are no subscriptions.

I bought UtilityBox on another Mac. How do I restore it?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the app and choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen.

The network scanner finds no devices.

Make sure local network access is allowed for UtilityBox in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network, and that you are connected to Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

The voice recorder says it cannot access the microphone.

Enable UtilityBox in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone, then reopen the app.

Does UtilityBox send my files or scan results anywhere?

No. Tools run on your Mac. A few tools contact an online service only when you use them; see the Privacy Policy for the full list.

Where do converted files go?

To the location you choose, or the Downloads or Music folder when macOS does not allow writing next to the original file.
