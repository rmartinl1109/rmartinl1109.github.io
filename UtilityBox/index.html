v1.0.0 September 2026 • Initial Production Suite

UtilityBox 1.0 — 10 High-Performance Native Utilities

First stable release engineered exclusively for macOS 14+ Sonoma and macOS 15+ Sequoia. Designed from scratch with pure Swift 6, reactive `@Observable` architecture, native Darwin sockets, Mach kernel interfaces, and full App Sandbox compliance with zero root requirements.

✨ 10 Implemented Utilities
  • LAN Network Scanner: High-concurrency /24 CIDR sweep, Bonjour mDNS discovery, and direct jump to Ping diagnostics.
  • Ping Diagnostic Tool: Non-root ICMP datagram sockets and TCP RTT modes with live Swift Charts latency curve and jitter tracking.
  • Port Checker & Auditor: BSD non-blocking sockets with poll() multiplexing and active banner grabbing (SSH, HTTP, FTP, MySQL).
  • DNS Lookup & Inspector: Comprehensive multi-record queries (A, AAAA, MX, TXT, SOA, CAA), global propagation, recursive trace, and raw dig console.
  • Speed Test Benchmark: Cloudflare Edge Anycast multi-thread benchmark, loaded latency bufferbloat scoring (A+ to F), and speedometer gauge.
  • Hardware & System Monitor: Mach microkernel telemetry, per-core P-Cores/E-Cores load grid, unified memory pressure, and top processes.
  • Disk Analyzer & Cleaner: Interactive APFS Treemap, Large Files Hunter (>50MB to >1GB), and safe developer/browser cache cleaner.
  • Hi-Fi Audio Converter & Studio: Batch audio conversion (M4A, ALAC, FLAC 24-bit, WAV, MP3 with ID3v2.3), -1 dBFS volume normalization, and A/B waveform player.
  • Interactive Unit Converter: Real-time dual sliders, IEC binary vs SI decimal storage, UI density points, and download time estimator.
  • QR Code Studio & Scanner: Stylized vector generation, SF Symbols center icons, and Apple Vision offline barcode decoding.
⚡ Core Architecture & Experience
  • Engineered with Swift 6 and strict concurrency safety throughout.
  • Zero third-party dependencies: 100% native Darwin, Network.framework, and Apple system APIs.
  • Full multilingual localization across 6 languages (English default, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese).
  • Cinematic splash screen presentation, 3D squircle production icon set, and Favorites & Recents quick-access hubs.

Engineering Foundation

Architected for Speed, Security & Elegance

How UtilityBox achieves maximum performance without compromising security or relying on bulky third-party dependencies.

Swift 6 & Observation

Built using Apple's cutting-edge `@Observable` macro and `@MainActor` thread-safety guarantees. Eliminates Combine boilerplate, reduces memory overhead, and ensures snappy UI updates at 120Hz ProMotion.

  • Granular view invalidation with zero lag
  • Structured Swift Concurrency (`async/await`, `withTaskGroup`)
  • Data-race safety enabled throughout the codebase

App Sandbox Compliant

Full system and network audit capabilities achieved strictly within Apple's App Sandbox. Employs non-privileged ICMP datagram sockets, non-blocking TCP connect/poll probes, and Mach kernel APIs without needing `sudo`.

  • Compliant with macOS 14 Sonoma & 15 Sequoia policies
  • `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription` privacy enforcement
  • Ready for Mac App Store or Apple Notarized distribution

Low-Level Darwin & Mach APIs

Direct access to the macOS XNU kernel interfaces including `host_processor_info`, `vm_statistics64`, `getifaddrs`, and `sysctl` gives microsecond telemetry precision that shell scripts simply cannot match.

  • Accurate Apple Silicon P-Core vs E-Core load breakdown
  • Real-time unified memory pressure & compression telemetry
  • Fast non-blocking socket pools for 254-host CIDR sweeps

Full Internationalization

Engineered from the ground up with multi-lingual support in 6 world languages. Live dynamic switching via `LocalizationManager.shared` without restarting the app.

  • English (Default), Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese
  • Consistent typography across all locales
  • Quick toggle in Settings (`Cmd + ,`)

Designed Exclusively for the Modern Mac

UtilityBox integrates seamlessly with macOS design principles: unified toolbar, translucent sidebars, Swift Charts visualizations, native drag-and-drop, and high-fidelity dark mode.
