Engineering Foundation

Architected for Speed, Security & Elegance

How UtilityBox achieves maximum performance without compromising security or relying on bulky third-party dependencies.

Swift 6 & Observation

Built using Apple's cutting-edge `@Observable` macro and `@MainActor` thread-safety guarantees. Eliminates Combine boilerplate, reduces memory overhead, and ensures snappy UI updates at 120Hz ProMotion.

  • Granular view invalidation with zero lag
  • Structured Swift Concurrency (`async/await`, `withTaskGroup`)
  • Data-race safety enabled throughout the codebase

App Sandbox Compliant

Full system and network audit capabilities achieved strictly within Apple's App Sandbox. Employs non-privileged ICMP datagram sockets, non-blocking TCP connect/poll probes, and Mach kernel APIs without needing `sudo`.

  • Compliant with macOS 14 Sonoma & 15 Sequoia policies
  • `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription` privacy enforcement
  • Ready for Mac App Store or Apple Notarized distribution

Low-Level Darwin & Mach APIs

Direct access to the macOS XNU kernel interfaces including `host_processor_info`, `vm_statistics64`, `getifaddrs`, and `sysctl` gives microsecond telemetry precision that shell scripts simply cannot match.

  • Accurate Apple Silicon P-Core vs E-Core load breakdown
  • Real-time unified memory pressure & compression telemetry
  • Fast non-blocking socket pools for 254-host CIDR sweeps

Full Internationalization

Engineered from the ground up with multi-lingual support in 6 world languages. Live dynamic switching via `LocalizationManager.shared` without restarting the app.

  • English (Default), Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese
  • Consistent typography across all locales
  • Quick toggle in Settings (`Cmd + ,`)

Designed Exclusively for the Modern Mac

UtilityBox integrates seamlessly with macOS design principles: unified toolbar, translucent sidebars, Swift Charts visualizations, native drag-and-drop, and high-fidelity dark mode.
