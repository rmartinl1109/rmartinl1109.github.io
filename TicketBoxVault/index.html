Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TicketBox Vault

How does on-device OCR protect my financial privacy?

Unlike other receipt apps that upload your physical receipts and bank cards to external third-party cloud servers, TicketBox Vault processes everything locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework and Neural Engine. No third party ever sees your merchant names, totals, or payment proofs.

What is a Claim PDF Dossier?

When an electronic device breaks, or when an online store refuses to honor a warranty, you need clear documentation. TicketBox Vault compiles a PDF with product details, purchase date, merchant, price, warranty status, serial number, and a high-resolution capture of your receipt that you can share with the retailer or manufacturer.

Is TicketBox Vault Pro really a one-time purchase?

Yes, absolutely. We hate subscription fatigue as much as you do. For a single one-time price of $4.99 USD (4,99 € in Europe), you unlock unlimited storage and all Pro features permanently. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, supports Family Sharing for up to 5 family members, and can be restored anytime on new devices.

Which languages and platforms are natively supported?

TicketBox Vault is built natively with SwiftUI for iOS 17+ and iPadOS 17+. It is fully localized in 6 official languages: English, Spanish, French, German, European Portuguese (pt-PT), and Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans).

Can I import receipts directly from files or the Share Sheet?

Yes. You can scan physical receipts using your camera, import photos from your Photos library, or share PDF receipts and images directly into TicketBox Vault from Mail, Safari, or the Files app via the native iOS Share Sheet.
