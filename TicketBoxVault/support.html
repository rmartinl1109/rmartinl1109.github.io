Frequently Encountered Questions

  • How do I restore my Lifetime Pro purchase?
    Open TicketBox Vault > tap Settings > tap "Restore Purchases". Ensure your device is connected to the internet and signed in with the Apple ID used to make the initial purchase.
  • Why is the OCR scanner not detecting a faded receipt?
    Thermal receipts often fade under heat or sunlight. Flatten the paper on a dark, non-reflective background and ensure adequate indirect lighting. You can always tap on any detected field to manually adjust the merchant name, date, or total amount.
  • How do I sync receipts between iPhone and Mac?
    TicketBox Vault uses your personal Apple iCloud via CloudKit. Ensure iCloud Drive is enabled in device Settings (Apple ID > iCloud > TicketBox Vault) on all devices.
  • How do I add a Home Screen or Lock Screen Widget?
    Long press on your iPhone Home Screen until the apps jiggle > tap the "+" button in the top corner > search for "TicketBox Vault" > choose between Return Window Alert or Warranty Countdown widgets.
