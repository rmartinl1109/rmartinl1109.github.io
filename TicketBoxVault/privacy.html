Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Apple ID: 6823956977

Privacy by Design: TicketBox Vault is engineered so your financial records, purchase tickets, receipt images, and warranty data stay entirely on your physical device or inside your private, encrypted Apple iCloud account. We never sell your personal data, nor do we track you across apps or services.

1. Information We Collect & How It Is Processed

Our architecture follows the principle of data minimization and strict on-device computation:

  • Receipt & Invoice Content (On-Device OCR): When you scan a receipt using your camera or import a document from your photo library or files, all text recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on your device using Apple's native Vision framework and Neural Engine. No receipt images, text extracts, merchant names, or financial totals are ever transmitted to any external server.
  • Local Data Storage: Receipts, warranty dates, return countdowns, and notes are saved locally on your device within your app sandbox, protected by iOS/macOS Complete File Protection (.completeFileProtection) hardware encryption.
  • iCloud Sync (CloudKit): When enabled, your records sync seamlessly across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit. Data in transit and at rest in iCloud is encrypted by Apple using your personal Apple ID account credentials. We have no access to your iCloud data.
  • Crash Diagnostics (Opt-in): If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers through iOS/macOS Settings, we receive anonymized, aggregated crash reports via Apple's Xcode Organizer to help eliminate bugs.

2. Biometrics & Keychain Security

When you enable Biometric Lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID), authentication is handled exclusively by Apple's hardware Secure Enclave and LocalAuthentication APIs. TicketBox Vault never captures, stores, or accesses your mathematical biometric geometry.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

TicketBox Vault contains zero third-party tracking SDKs, zero advertising networks, and zero behavioral analytics scripts. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by third parties.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

Transactions for TicketBox Lifetime Pro are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit and your Apple ID. We do not process, collect, or store your credit card or financial billing information.

5. Your Rights & Data Export

You retain 100% ownership of your data at all times. You can export individual legal claim dossiers as signed PDFs, backup your receipts, or delete any record instantly from the app.

6. Changes to This Policy

Any revisions to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. Any structural change involving user data will also be declared in the application's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest and App Store Connect Privacy Nutrition Labels.

7. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding your privacy, please visit our Support Page.
