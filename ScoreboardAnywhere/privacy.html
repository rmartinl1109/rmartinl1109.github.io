Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Privacy by Design: Scoreboard Anywhere has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your players and matches stay on your device. We do not collect any data about you.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal data. The developer never receives your players, your matches or any information about how you use the app. Accordingly, the app declares "Data Not Collected" in its App Store privacy details.

  • On-Device Data: The players you save, your matches and your rounds are stored only on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • Your Display Name: The name you choose when sharing or joining a match is remembered on your device (using the system's standard preferences storage) so you don't have to type it again. It is never sent to the developer.
  • App Review Prompt: The app keeps a small counter of finished matches on your device to decide when to politely ask for an App Store rating. Nothing is sent anywhere.

2. Sharing a Match With Nearby Devices

If you choose to share a match, the app uses Apple's peer-to-peer networking (MultipeerConnectivity) over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. The match is sent directly between the devices at your table, encrypted, with no server and no internet connection involved. The host approves every device that asks to join. When you share or look for a nearby match, iOS asks for permission to use the local network; if you decline, the rest of the app keeps working.

A device that joins keeps a copy of the match you shared, including the players' names and the points entered, until its owner deletes it.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

The app includes no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks and no tracking of any kind. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. The "More apps" screen only opens App Store pages of the developer's other apps; the app itself does not contact any server to show it.

4. Deleting Your Data

There is no account to delete. You can delete any match from the Ongoing or History lists, and saved players from the new-match screen. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

5. Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children, and contains no advertising or links to the open web other than the App Store.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date. Any change that introduces data collection will be reflected at the same time in the app's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy manifest and in the App Store privacy details.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy, contact us via our Support Page.
