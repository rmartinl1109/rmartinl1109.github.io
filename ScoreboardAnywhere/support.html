Common Troubleshooting Steps

  • I can't see the other device when sharing: Make sure both devices have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on (they don't need to be on the same network), are close to each other and run the same version of the app. If iOS asked for "Local Network" access and you declined, enable it in Settings > Scoreboard Anywhere.
  • The connection dropped: The guest device tries to reconnect on its own for a minute. If it doesn't come back, tap Reconnect in the match, or Stop following to keep the copy as your own match. Keep the screen on during a match: locking the device interrupts sharing.
  • I can't add points to a shared match: Only the host's match is the original. If a copy says it is offline, reconnect first; offline copies can't add points so that matches never get out of sync.
  • I entered a wrong number: Open the menu of that round and choose Edit points or Delete round.
  • Language: The app follows your device language (Settings > General > Language & Region).
