What it does

Everything a score keeper should be

Four scoring styles, one calm and fast interface, built natively for iPhone and iPad.

Points by Rounds

Add each round, see the full table and the winner. Set a points limit and choose whether the most or the fewest points win.

Darts Countdown

Start from 301, 501 or your own total. Exact zero wins, and a bust never counts. The scoreboard always shows what each player has left.

Simple Counter & Sets

Tap + or − for quick counts, or play best-of-N sets for padel, table tennis and volleyball.

Share With Nearby Devices

Everyone follows and adds points from their own iPhone or iPad. The host approves each person. It works without internet or an account.

Fix Any Round

Typed a wrong number? Correct the points of any round or delete it; the rounds that follow renumber themselves. Shared matches stay in sync.

History & Saved Players

Your players are remembered for the next game, ongoing matches wait for you and finished ones are kept in your history.

Private by Design

No account, no ads and no analytics. Matches live on your device and travel only between the devices you choose to share with.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

Scoreboard Anywhere runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS and iPadOS 26 or later.

How does sharing a match work?

One device hosts the match and becomes visible to nearby iPhones and iPads. Whoever wants to join asks to enter and the host approves them. Everyone can then add points, and all screens update. It uses Apple's peer-to-peer networking, so it needs neither internet nor an account. Everyone should have the same app version.

What happens if the connection drops?

The guest's device keeps a copy of the match and automatically tries to reconnect to the host for a minute. While it is offline it cannot add points, so two versions of a match never diverge. If it doesn't come back, you can reconnect by hand or stop following and keep the copy as your own match.

Do I need an account? Is my data collected?

No account and no data collection. Players and matches are stored only on your device. When you share a match, the data travels directly between the devices involved, encrypted and without any server. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I correct a mistake in an earlier round?

Yes. Each round has a menu to edit its points or delete it, and the following rounds are renumbered. In a darts countdown everything is recalculated, including busts and the winner.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (European and Brazilian), Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. The app follows your device language.
