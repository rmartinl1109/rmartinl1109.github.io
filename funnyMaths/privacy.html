Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: Funny Maths does not collect any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What a child practises stays on the device.

1. Who we are

Funny Maths is developed by Roberto Martin Lopez ("we", "us"). You can reach us at funnymaths.support@rmartinl1109.com or through the support page.

2. What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following information only on your device:

  • Practice history: which operations were answered, whether each answer was right or wrong, and on which day.
  • Settings: your choices for tables, exercises and the Trainer, such as the number of digits.
  • Child profiles (Funny Maths Pro): the names and icons you choose for each child, and the practice history of each profile.

None of this is sent to us or to anybody else, and the app does not sync it between your devices. Like the data of any app, it can be included in the device backups you make with iCloud Backup or with a computer; those backups are handled by Apple and we have no access to them.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, advertising identifier or usage analytics. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. The app connects to the internet only to talk to the App Store about purchases.

4. Purchases

Funny Maths Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store, on your device, whether you own Funny Maths Pro. You can restore a previous purchase at any time with the Restore Purchases button on the Pro screen.

5. Children

Funny Maths is made for children who are learning maths, and it does not collect personal information from anybody, children included. Names typed into child profiles stay on the device. A parent can delete a profile, together with its practice history, from the Profiles screen, and deleting the app removes all the data it stored.

6. Earlier versions

Versions of Funny Maths before 2.0 showed advertising served by Google through Google Mobile Ads, which handles data under Google's own policies. Version 2.0 and later contain no ads and no advertising software.

7. Diagnostics from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. They contain no personal information and we use them only to fix bugs.

8. Your choices

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in control of what the app stores: delete a child profile, or delete the app. If you have a question about privacy, write to us.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be published on this page with its date. Any change that involved collecting data would also be reflected in the privacy information of the app in the App Store.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: funnymaths.support@rmartinl1109.com.
