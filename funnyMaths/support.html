Common questions

  • Pro is not unlocked after buying it: open the Pro screen (the Pro button at the top right of the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases. Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it and that the device is online.
  • I bought Pro in an earlier version: it is the same purchase and includes the new features. Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen if it does not show as active.
  • How do I add a child? With Pro, tap the profile at the top left of the home screen, choose a name and an icon, and tap Add Profile. Each child keeps their own progress.
  • Where is my child's progress? Only on the device where the app was used. It is not shared between devices, and deleting the app removes it.
  • Language: the app follows your device language. You can also change it for this app only in Settings > Funny Maths > Language.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
