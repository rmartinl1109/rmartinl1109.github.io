Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Which devices does Funny Maths run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. On iPad in landscape, exercises use two columns so everything fits on screen without scrolling.

What is free and what is Pro?

Tables, the quiz, sums and subtractions in columns, the Trainer, My Progress and the review are free. Funny Maths Pro adds long multiplication and division, profiles for up to six children and the Parent Dashboard.

Is there a subscription?

No. Funny Maths Pro is a single in-app purchase. It does not renew, and you can restore it with the Restore Purchases button on the Pro screen.

I bought Pro in an earlier version. Do I keep it?

Yes. It is the same purchase and it includes the new Pro features at no extra cost. If the app does not show Pro as active, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

What happens with my child's data?

Funny Maths has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not send what your child practises anywhere. Progress and profile names are stored only on the device and are not shared between devices. Read the full privacy policy.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. The internet is only used by the App Store when you buy or restore Pro.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, European Portuguese and Simplified Chinese. The app follows the language of your device.

What happened to the ads?

Version 2.0 is a complete rebuild and has no ads at all. Earlier versions showed ads, and Pro used to remove them.
