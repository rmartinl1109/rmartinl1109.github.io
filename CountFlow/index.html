Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is CountFlow free? How does the Pro upgrade work?

CountFlow is completely free to download and includes 1 active counter forever with all core tracking and calendar tools included. When you want to track more activities, a single, one-time lifetime In-App Purchase (CountFlow Pro) unlocks unlimited counters, all categories, and future updates with no recurring subscription fees.

Does CountFlow work without internet connection?

Yes, completely. All records are persisted locally on your device via Apple's native SwiftData framework. It works reliably on flights, underground subways, remote trails, or gyms with zero signal.

Can I change counts for past or future days?

Yes! Just tap on any day in the monthly calendar to open the quick editor. You can update the count, increment or decrement it, or clear the day at any time.

Which languages are supported?

CountFlow is natively localized in 6 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese. It automatically matches your device language.

Does CountFlow support Apple TV (tvOS)?

Yes! CountFlow is a Universal Purchase app. You can install it on your Apple TV 4K or Apple TV HD directly from the tvOS App Store. It connects seamlessly to your iCloud database, offers full Siri Remote focus navigation, and features an Ambient Kiosk mode for 10-foot tracking in your home or workspace.

How does the per-counter analytics filter work?

Unlike generic habit apps that lump all numbers together, CountFlow filters Swift Charts analytics strictly by individual counter. Select any counter (such as Water, Workouts, or Reading) to inspect its specific 7, 30, or 90-day progress, daily average, and all-time record without mixing unrelated activities.

What Apple platforms and OS versions do I need?

CountFlow requires iOS 17.0+ on iPhone, iPadOS 17.0+ on iPad, and tvOS 17.0+ on Apple TV. It is engineered natively in Swift 6 with SwiftData and CloudKit for instantaneous responsiveness and zero battery drain.

What features are planned for future updates?

Our active roadmap includes Siri Shortcuts & Action Button integration, daily reminder notifications, calendar day notes, CSV/PDF export, specialized dual-pane and tabletop layouts for the foldable iPhone Duo, and native watchOS and macOS experiences. Check our Roadmap section above for details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

CountFlow does not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any of your personal information, counter logs, habit history, or device identifiers. All your counters, records, and preferences are stored locally on your device via Apple's native SwiftData framework.

2. Private iCloud Synchronization

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your counter data is synchronized across your personal Apple devices using Apple's private CloudKit database.
