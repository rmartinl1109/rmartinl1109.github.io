Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is CountFlow free? How does the Pro upgrade work?

CountFlow is completely free to download and comes with up to 2 active counters forever with all core tracking and calendar tools included. When you want to track more activities, a single, one-time lifetime In-App Purchase (CountFlow Pro) unlocks unlimited counters, all categories, and future updates with no recurring subscription fees.

Does CountFlow work without internet connection?

Yes, completely. All records are persisted locally on your device via Apple's native SwiftData framework. It works reliably on flights, underground subways, remote trails, or gyms with zero signal.

Can I change counts for past or future days?

Yes! Just tap on any day in the monthly calendar to open the quick editor. You can update the count, increment or decrement it, or clear the day at any time.

Which languages are supported?

CountFlow is natively localized in 6 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese. It automatically matches your device language.

What iOS version do I need?

CountFlow is engineered for iOS 17.0 and later, utilizing Apple's modern SwiftUI and SwiftData architecture for maximum fluidity and minimal battery consumption.
