Privacy Policy

Your Privacy is Fundamental. Final Countdown is designed from the ground up to respect your personal privacy:

  • Zero Tracking: We do not track, collect, or transmit your personal data, events, or usage metrics to any third party.
  • On-Device Storage: All event titles, notes, and dates are stored locally on your device using native Apple persistent frameworks.
  • iCloud Sync: When enabled, event data syncs directly through Apple’s secure iCloud Key-Value store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore), encrypted and protected under your Apple ID.
  • Calendar Access: If granted, calendar access is used strictly on-device via EventKit to import chosen events into your countdown list.
